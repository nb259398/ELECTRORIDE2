How it works

Get from A to B in four simple steps.

🗺️

1. Plan your route

Enter where you are and where you want to go. We find your best trip.

💳

2. Pay with MoMoPay

Mobile money, card, or QR code. Your ticket arrives in seconds.

🚍

3. Board at your stage

Live arrival times on the app. You only wait as long as you need to.

👤

4. Board off and rate us

Get off at your stop and take 10 seconds to rate your journey.
