About ELECTRORIDE

Welcome to ELECTRORIDE This is an innovative, sustainable public e-transit pilot project engineered to revolutionize urban mobility by optimizing clean public transportation networks. To support the technical lifecycle of zero-emission electric fleets, the project integrates real-time positioning of charging stations alongside convenient, affordable, and inclusive digital payment systems, while facilitating transparent community communication through instantaneous customer and ride-status feedback loops. By transitioning mass transit reliance toward electric vehicles, the platform advances SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) through reduced fossil fuel dependency. Concurrently, its smart architecture directly embodies SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure) by leveraging state-of-the-art digital infrastructure to foster resilient, climate-smart transit operations. Ultimately, by offering accessible, efficient, and low-carbon alternative travel options, it acts as a vital catalyst for SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities), mitigating urban congestion, reducing localized air pollution, and establishing a scalable blueprint for equitable municipal development over the next decade.

🚍 Mission

To simplify, modernize, and streamline transport management for passengers and administrators.

⚡ Challenge

• Uncoordinated routes, poor scheduling, and uncertain arrival times, Fluctuating fuel prices or having to service their petrol bike ever so often, Manual ticketing leading to long queues and revenue leakage, Limited data for authorities to plan and regulate transport effectively, Safety concerns with unmonitored driver behavior and vehicle conditions. .

💡 Solution

Online booking, real-time trip management, modern dashboards, and secure database integration.

🎯 Vision

A smarter, greener, and more connected transport system for the future.

Developed by Nsobya Bosco, Tukamushaba Ruth, Christabella Buberwa, Ahmad Umar Rufai, Nsegera Matia, Bachelor of IT students at Cavendish University Uganda, ELECTRORIDE combines academic learning with practical system development.
